## Future Evolution of the Rainy Season in Mali under CMIP6 Climate Projections
#Objective
This project investigates future changes in the rainy season over Mali using CMIP6 climate projections.

# Study Area
Mali

# Climate Models
ACCESS-CM2
MPI-ESM1-2-HR
Climate Scenarios
Historical (1985–2014)
SSP126
SSP245
SSP585
# Methods
# The analysis includes:

Spatial averaging of precipitation over Mali
Rainy season onset determination
Rainy season cessation determination
Rainy season duration calculation
Historical and future scenario comparison
